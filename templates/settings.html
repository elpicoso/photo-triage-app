Anthropic model ID used for reject/rating/keyword suggestions.
Images sent per API call. Anthropic caps requests at 100 images for 200k-context models (the Haiku default) or 600 for 1M-context models (Opus, Sonnet 5, Fable 5) — but the 32MB total request-size limit is usually the real ceiling first, which is why previews are downscaled (see Max preview dimension). Batches above 20 images also require each image to stay under 2000px on its long edge.
Long edge, in pixels, previews are downscaled to before sending. The API hard-rejects any image over 8000px, but Claude itself only uses up to 1568px (standard models) or 2576px (Claude 4.7+ high-resolution tier) — anything larger than your model's tier is wasted bandwidth, not added fidelity.
